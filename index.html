Mars clock--:--

The time right now at Mars’ “Greenwich” line (its prime meridian). Scientists call it Coordinated Mars Time, or MTC.

Mars day number…

A Mars day is called a sol. It lasts 24 hours and 40 minutes. This counts every sol since December 29, 1873, like a calendar for Mars.

Distance from Earth…

Earth and Mars both travel around the Sun at different speeds, so this changes every day, from about 55 million to 400 million km.

Message travel time…

How long a radio message, moving at the speed of light, takes to get from Earth to Mars. That’s why rovers can’t be driven with a joystick!
